Visual guide: polar-line location (L)

Blue dashed lines indicate the upper and lower polar lines; the teal dashed line is a conceptual axial renal midline. These simplified diagrams illustrate the scoring definitions and are not a substitute for reviewing CT or MRI.

Original educational schematic. Apply the validated definitions to the appropriate imaging planes and local reporting protocol.

Interpretation: 4–6 = low complexity; 7–9 = intermediate complexity; 10–12 = high complexity. The numeric total is R + E + N + L; A is a non-numeric descriptor. Append “h” when hilar criteria are met. Use CT/MRI, not ultrasound. The score describes anatomical complexity and should support—not replace—clinical judgement.
