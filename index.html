Leibovich Score · Renal Cell Carcinoma

Opens the OncoToolkit implementation of the 2003 Leibovich model for postoperative, non-metastatic clear-cell RCC. It uses pathological T and N categories, maximum tumour diameter, Fuhrman grade and histological tumour necrosis. Use the original model's intended population and do not automatically substitute current WHO/ISUP grade for Fuhrman grade.
